  <h2 style="font-size: clamp(1.05rem, 3.4vw, 1.5rem); font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 0.75rem; white-space: nowrap;">Welcome to the Alternative Toxicology &amp; Analytics (ATA) Lab</h2>
  <p>We are the Alternative Toxicology & Analytics (ATA) Lab at Chungbuk National University, led by Dr. rer. nat. Hyunki Cho (조현기). Our research focuses on non-target chemical screening, New Approach Methodologies (NAMs)-based in vitro platforms, and multi-omics integration for chemical safety and pharmacological activity assessment, with practical applications including toxicity assessment of hazardous substances, metabolite profiling following chemical exposure, and screening of plant-derived and xenobiotic metabolites.
    
We are looking for passionate new members including undergraduate interns, MS, and PhD students to join the team.</p>
